# Product Principles

## Q: What do Kainnne products have in common?

- Intuitive interactions
- Consistent interfaces and behavior
- Simple, useful open-source tools
- Refined, minimal layouts
- A clear purpose at first glance

## Q: Why do product pages show only essential information?

- Users can understand the purpose and primary features quickly.
- Less time is spent reading, finding entry points, or learning the interface.
