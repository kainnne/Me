# AI and Work

## Q: What kinds of tasks do you currently complete with AI?

- AI system and application development
- Software development, testing, debugging, and deployment
- Documentation, data, and knowledge-base organization
- Research, data analysis, and experiment planning
- Training and learning-material development
- Project planning and workflow automation

## Q: How do you work with AI Agents?

- I am a power user of AI Agents, treating them as collaborators across long-term development, research, and project execution.
- I maintain multiple long-term projects at the same time, using AGENTS.md, Markdown, Project Contracts, Git history, and task handoffs to preserve context, memory, rules, and current state.
- I break large goals into verifiable research, implementation, testing, documentation, and deployment tasks; when appropriate, I have multiple Agents handle independent work in parallel.
- Before execution, I define the objective, file scope, protected areas, acceptance criteria, and existing decisions to reduce misunderstandings, rework, and wasted tokens.
- I automate recurring workflows such as builds, testing, deployment, HTTPS status checks, domain configuration tracking, documentation synchronization, and knowledge-base maintenance.
- I design rigorous Loop Engineering workflows to reduce the time spent writing prompts and focusing on one task at a time while keeping outcomes within controlled boundaries.
- I design system prompts that let me think in conversational language while turning that intent into precise work instructions.
